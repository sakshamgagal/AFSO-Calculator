AFSO Score Calculator

Advanced Fibrosis Severity in Obesity score for estimating the probability of advanced liver fibrosis in patients with obesity undergoing bariatric surgery.

Enhanced Liver Fibrosis score
Unit: ×10⁹/L
Liver stiffness measurement, kPa
Please enter valid positive values for ELF score, platelet count, and LSM.
Estimated probability of advanced fibrosis:

P = −13.3529 + (1.0708 × ELF) − (0.0102 × Platelets) + (1.5746 × ln(LSM))
AFSO = exp(P) / [1 + exp(P)]

This calculator is based on an internally validated prediction model and is intended for research and clinical decision-support purposes only. It should not replace clinical judgment, liver specialist assessment, or externally validated local pathways. External validation is required before routine clinical implementation.
